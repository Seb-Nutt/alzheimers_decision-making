When examining the effect of Alzhiemer's on the prisoners dilemma we can see how the deterioration of long-term decision making and critical thinking affects the individuals strategy. While a standard participant uses a strategy similar to a tit-for-tat with forgiveness approach, the long term benefits of higher returns stemming from continuous cooperation are forgotten within a participant under the effects of Alzhiemer's disease. The damage to long-term thinking leads to prioritising the short-term reward of potentially higher points via stealing, disrupting the relasionship between the two participants which can cause signifigant detrements to potential gains over the course of repeated simulations. This reflects the minds deterioration as a whole, as Alzhiemer's disease continues to impair and cripple proper decision-making methods.
