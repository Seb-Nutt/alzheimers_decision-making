# CS30 Major Project Reflection
## What advice would you give yourself if you were to start a project like this again?
If I were to give myself advice for another similar project I would remind myself to be ambitious and push my abilities as a programmer. I believe there are aspects of this project that I could have expanded on or attempted to implement a more complicated and efficient solution to some problems.
On the more cautionary side, I would also likely remind myself that not every system needs to be perfect. Especially early on I was hyperfocused with implementing the most elegant systems and trying to make the most visually appealing final product as possible. This ate up a lot of my time which ultimately led me to the exact opposite problem as my time for this project dwindled.
Finally, I would advise myself to make sure I am making at least some progress every day. During this project there was a stretch of time where I was stuck attempting to develop a complicated 3d net system which ultimately did not matter to my end product. If I were to encounter a problem like this in the future, I would take a lighter approach and sprinkle in progress aimed at resolving the issue and maintain consistent progress towards my end goal.

## Did you complete everything in your "needs to have" list?
I did complete everything in my needs to have, even progressing further and exceeding one of the goals. I had sufficient time to fully flesh out each of these goals while working towards my end product.
Along with my needs to have a list, I also worked towards my nice to have list. I completed a majority of my objectives, only stalling on the search bar due to my project differing from what I had in mind initially.
I was also able to implement and correct problems and possible issues that were pointed out by my beta testers to improve the quality of my final project. This allowed me to catch and improve on systems that I otherwise would not have noticed or improved.

## What was the hardest part of the project?
From a coding perspective, the model and implementing its systems such as the region selection mode was the most technically difficult aspect of the project. I had to run through multiple systems and look for creative solutions for ensuring the model was colored correct and rotated independently. It took around a week to find the system that I would use for coloring and information displaying which pushed my perseverance as I had been struggling for a while until that point.
The mental aspect of this stretch was very challenging due to the frustrating nature of bug fixing. Knowing that I was making no progress was very discouraging and heavily impacted my development process but without that struggle I would not have eventually succeeded in implementing a functional system that would be the foundation of my project.


## Were there any problems you could not solve?
The issue of scaling both the model and the webpage around it was an issue that had been in my mind across the duration of the project. The nature of using a predetermined system of nodes to achieve the desired visual effect was something that proved a difficult roadblock when trying to implement a scale, especially in webGL mode. This issue was slowly pushed to the backburner as I continued to  branch out further into my project.
